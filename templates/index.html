About Project

Urban Food Delivery Intelligence System — a DWM mini-project.

Problem Statement

Food delivery operations generate large operational data, but raw logs don't explain why delays happen or what to do. This system turns raw Zomato delivery records into a warehouse + OLAP cube + mining models that predict risk and recommend actions.

Objective & Flow

RAW → ETL → DATA WAREHOUSE (star schema, MySQL) → OLAP (roll-up/drill-down/slice/dice/pivot) → DATA MINING (Apriori, K-Means, RandomForest, IsolationForest) → PREDICTION → EXPLAINABLE INSIGHTS → RECOMMENDATIONS.

Dataset

allenborochin/zomato_delivery_EDA · zomato_cleaned.csv (~39K rows). Columns: delivery time, distance, weather, traffic, vehicle, city, order type, ratings, geo-coordinates, timestamps.

DWM Concepts Demonstrated

  • ETL with quality metrics
  • Star schema, surrogate keys, facts/dimensions
  • OLAP operations
  • Association rules (support/confidence/lift)
  • Clustering + profiling
  • Classification + confusion matrix
  • Anomaly detection

Unique Features

Risk Predictor · Why-Late explainer · Pattern discovery · Behavior clustering · Hotspots · What-if simulator · Smart recommendations · SLA monitor · Warehouse explorer.

Technologies

Flask, Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, mlxtend, MySQL, Chart.js, Leaflet, vanilla JS.

Future Scope

Real-time streaming, route optimization, demand forecasting, SHAP explanations.
